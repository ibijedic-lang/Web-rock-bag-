Why Rock Bags

The advantages of HDPE Rock Bag Filter Units

Every element of the unit — from the netting fibre to the lifting system — is engineered to survive harsh marine environments and outperform traditional armour on speed, cost and longevity.

Engineered HDPE netting

The netting fibres are manufactured from High Density Polyethylene (HDPE), designed to withstand the toughest marine conditions: prolonged saltwater immersion, UV radiation, abrasion and severe weather events.

  • UV-stabilised for exposed, above-water installations
  • Inert in saltwater and fresh water — no corrosion, unlike steel gabions
  • High abrasion resistance against mobile sediment and rock contact
  • Life expectancy of 25 to over 100 years depending on application
  • Safety factor of 6 on the lifting system for confident handling
