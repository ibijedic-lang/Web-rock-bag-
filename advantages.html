The Advantages of HDPE Rock Bag Filter Units

The netting is the difference between a unit that lasts a season and one that lasts a lifetime. Here's why we specify high-density polyethylene for demanding environments.

Higher Tensile Strength

HDPE has a higher tensile strength and greater resistance to environmental factors — UV radiation, temperature fluctuations and abrasion — than standard PE. That strength persists through decades of loading, movement and handling.

Superior UV Resistance

Our units contain carbon black, providing outstanding UV resistance so the netting maintains its strength and integrity over extended exposure to sunlight. HDPE with UV stabiliser achieves a 50–100 year design lifespan.

Up to 95% Less Microplastic

HDPE netting is far less prone to breaking down into microplastics than standard or recycled PE — reducing microplastic release by up to 95% and lowering the risk of pollution and harm to marine ecosystems.

High Abrasion Resistance

In comparison to geosynthetic bags and recycled PE, HDPE offers high abrasion resistance and very low material loss — critical where units sit on mobile beds, rock or concrete, or in sediment-laden flows.

Strength-to-Weight Ratio

HDPE marine netting is lightweight and flexible yet strong and durable — easy to transport and fill on site, yet capable of containing ten tonnes of rock through lifting, placement and decades of service.

Outlasts Recycled Materials

PE and HDPE outlast recycled material in all environments. For assets designed for a long service life, the netting specification should never be the weakest link.
