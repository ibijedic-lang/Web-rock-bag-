Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What sizes are available?
Rock Bag Filter Units are available in 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 tonne options depending on the application and the lifting equipment available on site.
Are units filled by weight or volume?
Units are filled by volume, not weight. The "tonne" rating is an identifier meaning the finished unit will on average fall within that weight range — actual weight varies with the rock density used.
How fast can units be installed?
A small team can efficiently install hundreds of units per day using standard lifting equipment — significantly faster and more economical than concrete, gabions or riprap.
Can they be used underwater and in saltwater?
Yes. Units are suitable for above-ground or sub-surface placement, in fresh or saline environments, and for temporary or long-term works. HDPE netting with UV stabiliser is recommended for coastlines and high-energy water.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes — we supply worldwide from our Queensland base, with deployment support, technical assistance and filling-station options available for your project.
