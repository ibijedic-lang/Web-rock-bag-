About Us

Global supply, engineered in Australia

Rock Bag Filter Units is the dedicated rock bag brand of Project Material Pty Ltd — a leader in environmental and infrastructure protection products and services, based in Brendale, Queensland and supplying projects worldwide.

Five years of development. A product built to last a century.

Rock Bag Filter Units have been developed over a five-year period, with significant financial investment in product development and material testing — proving the concept as viable for designers and establishing manufacturing for the global market.

The result is a flexible yet robust unit that has rapidly become the preferred choice for erosion control and scour protection, flash flooding response, asset protection, coastlines, sand nourishment, ports, harbours and a growing range of applications across the construction and environmental markets.

  • Dedicated rock bag specialists — it's all we do
  • Design guidance and technical data for engineers
  • Deployment support from filling to final placement
  • Manufacturing scaled for worldwide project supply
