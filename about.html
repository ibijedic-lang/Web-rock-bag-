About Rock Bag Filter Units

Five years of product development, material testing and real-world deployment — built into every unit we supply.

Our Story

Engineered in Queensland, supplied worldwide

Rock Bag Filter Units has been developed over a five-year period with significant financial investment in product development and material testing. What began as a better answer to local erosion problems has grown into a product supplied to projects around the world.

The flexible yet robust rock bags have rapidly become popular for erosion control and scour protection, flash flooding, asset protection, coastlines, sand nourishment, ports, harbours and many other emerging applications.

We're based in Brendale, Queensland, and support every supply with deployment assistance, technical guidance and filling-station options — so your crew can produce and place units efficiently from day one.

Milestones

  • Product development begins

    Significant investment into design, netting materials and manufacturing methods.

  • Material testing programme

    Tensile, UV and abrasion testing to validate netting performance across environments.

  • Full-scale trials

    Full-scale trials of rock bag armour units prove stability in high-energy conditions.

  • Worldwide supply

    Units shipped globally with deployment support and technical assistance for every project.
