Applications

Environmental and infrastructure protection

Rock Bag Filter Units are supplied worldwide for erosion, scour and asset protection across the construction, environmental and energy sectors — above ground or sub-surface, fresh or saline, temporary or permanent.

Coastal Defence

Shoreline erosion control and wave-energy absorption on exposed coastlines. Units mould to the beach profile, retain sand nourishment and resist storm events that displace loose riprap.

Ports & Harbours

Quay wall toe protection, slope stabilisation and scour control around berths and breakwaters — installed quickly between vessel movements with minimal disruption to operations.

Bridge Pylons & Foundations

Local scour protection around piers, pylons and abutments in rivers and estuaries. Flexible units bed into scour holes and stop further undermining of critical foundations.

Culverts & Spillways

Inlet and outlet protection where concentrated flows attack the bed. Permeable rock bags dissipate energy while allowing water to pass, preventing outflanking and headcut erosion.

Offshore Wind Foundations

Scour protection and cable stabilisation around monopiles and jacket foundations. Full-scale trials have proven rock bag armour performance in offshore conditions.

Oil & Gas Pipelines

Pipeline crossing supports, span rectification and stabilisation on the seabed. Units conform to the pipe and bed geometry without damaging coatings.

Flash Flooding & Emergency Works

Rapid-response protection for embankments, levees and washed-out infrastructure. Bags fill with any locally available rock and deploy within hours of arrival on site.

River & Estuary Works

Bank protection, weir aprons, bed sills and temporary diversion works in fresh-water environments — removable and reusable when used for temporary construction phases.

Asset & Utility Protection

Ballast and cover for subsea cables, intakes, outfalls and other buried or exposed assets that need robust, inspectable long-term protection.
