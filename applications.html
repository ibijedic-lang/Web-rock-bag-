Rock Bag Applications

Flexible yet robust, Rock Bag Filter Units have rapidly become popular for erosion control and scour protection, flash flooding, asset protection, coastlines, sand nourishment, ports, harbours and many other emerging applications.

Coastal Defence & Shorelines

Rock bags attenuate wave energy to protect shorelines, beaches, dunes, tidal inlets and sand-nourishment sites. Their flexible, habitat-friendly construction works with the coast rather than against it, unlike rigid structures.

Bridge Pier & Abutment Scour

Bridge pylons and foundations are shielded from fierce currents and sediment displacement. Units conform tightly to the pier base and existing scour holes, arresting further undermining without dewatering the site.

Rivers, Culverts & Flood Response

Stabilise waterway infrastructure such as culverts, spillways and causeways — especially in flood- and erosion-prone zones. Rapid deployment makes rock bags ideal for emergency flash-flood response and temporary river control.

Ports & Harbours

Toe protection, revetment reinforcement and scour resistance around pylons, wharves and quay walls. Units are stable in propeller wash and turbulent berthing conditions, above or below the waterline.

Offshore Wind & Foundations

Rock bags secure monopiles and prevent scour under dynamic marine conditions at offshore wind-turbine foundations — a proven, removable alternative to rock dumping around subsea structures.

Subsea Cables & Pipelines

Stabilise subsea installations such as oil and gas pipelines and power or communications cables, safeguarding essential infrastructure from movement, scour and abrasion.

Asset Protection

Protect intakes, outfalls, jetty piles and other critical assets from scour and impact. Units can be installed sub-surface or above ground, in fresh or saline water, as temporary or long-term works.

Environmental & Habitat Works

The voids between rocks allow plants and marine animals to colonise the units, making rock bags well suited to environmentally sensitive sites, reef enhancement and habitat-creation projects.

Why It Works

Engineered to stay put

Rock Bag Filter Units effectively absorb wave energy and remain stable in turbulent water. The mesh keeps the rock mass acting as a single flexible unit that settles into scour holes and moulds itself to structures — maintaining protection where rigid systems crack, slump or wash out.
