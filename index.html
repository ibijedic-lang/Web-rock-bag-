The product

What is a Rock Bag Filter Unit?

A high-strength mesh net, machine-made from virgin, high-grade yarn with UV stabiliser, filled on site with locally sourced aggregate. The result is a heavy, flexible unit that moulds to the seabed or riverbed, absorbs wave and current energy, and stays put where loose rock and rigid structures fail.

Filled on site, fast

Bags are rapidly filled with a standard excavator, directly on site or at a laydown area before transport. One filling frame, one machine, one small crew.

Flexible by design

Each unit deforms to match the surface beneath it, interlocking with neighbouring bags to form a stable, permeable armour layer that dissipates energy instead of deflecting it.

Proven at full scale

Validated by the world-first full-scale rock bag stability test, conducted in Brisbane, Australia in 2022 — giving designers real engineering data, not just claims.

PE

Polyethylene

The workhorse for sub-surface installations — pipeline and cable protection, riverbeds, and permanently submerged scour aprons.

  • High-grade virgin PE yarn
  • Ideal for permanently submerged works
  • Economical for large sub-sea coverage
HDPE

High-Density Polyethylene

For above-ground and UV-exposed environments — coastlines, high-energy water, temporary works, mangroves and other sensitive locations.

  • Virgin HDPE with UV stabiliser and carbon black — not coated after production
  • 50–100 year design life
  • Higher tensile strength; superior abrasion, UV and temperature resistance
  • Up to 95% lower microplastic release than standard PE
  • KIWA, BTTG and DNV certified

Our unique design

Not another recycled-PET rock bag

Most filter unit rock bags on the market are knitted from 100% recycled polyester (PET). Ours are different by design: every unit is made from virgin HDPE yarn with UV stabiliser and carbon black built into the yarn itself — not coated on afterwards — and independently certified by KIWA, BTTG and DNV.

Standard layout

Single certified lifting ring with internal vertical reinforcement ropes running through the bag. One-point lifts for fast, accurate placement with an excavator or crane — the everyday layout for erosion control, scour aprons and emergency works.

S-Type layout

A heavy-lift rope layout for the largest tonnages and marine plant. Engineered for repeated lifts, controlled subsea release and deployment from vessels — the layout of choice for offshore wind, cables and pipeline protection.

Applications

One system. Every scour and erosion problem.

Deployed above ground or sub-surface, in fresh or saline water, for temporary or long-term works — routine programmes and emergency response alike.

Bridge piers & abutments

Shield structural supports from fierce currents and sediment displacement. Flexible units settle into scour holes and stop them growing.

Offshore wind foundations

Secure monopiles and jackets against scour under dynamic marine conditions, with rapid vessel-based deployment.

Subsea cables & pipelines

Stabilise, weigh down and protect subsea infrastructure from movement, abrasion and third-party damage.

Coastlines & sand nourishment

Absorb wave energy on exposed coastlines, anchor nourishment works and protect dunes and revetments.

Flood & emergency response

Fill and place hundreds of units in days — a rapid, re-deployable answer to flash flooding and storm damage.

Culverts, spillways & causeways

Stabilise waterway infrastructure in flood- and erosion-prone zones without dewatering or heavy civil works.

Ports & harbours

Quay wall toe protection, propeller wash scour repair and berth pocket stabilisation, stable in turbulent water.

Mangroves & sensitive environments

A permeable, low-impact armour that supports marine growth — HDPE units suit the most sensitive settings.

Temporary works & asset protection

Cofferdam scour aprons, outfall protection, plant crossings — install fast, recover and reuse when done.

Why rock bags

Faster and more economical than concrete or gabions

01

Speed of installation

A small team can fill and place more than 100 bags in a single day — no formwork, no curing, no specialist trades.

02

Minimal equipment

A standard excavator and lifting frame is all it takes. Units are placed accurately even in flowing or turbid water.

Permeable units absorb wave and current energy rather than deflecting it, staying stable in turbulent water and preventing edge scour.

04

Environmentally considered

Ethically sourced materials, locally won rock fill, marine-growth-friendly mesh, and HDPE that cuts microplastic release by up to 95%.

05

Recoverable & reusable

Lift, move and redeploy units as works progress — ideal for temporary schemes and staged construction.
