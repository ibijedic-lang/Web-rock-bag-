The Product

What is a Rock Bag Filter Unit?

A simple, proven idea: locally sourced rock, contained in a precision-engineered mesh net with a certified central lifting ring. The result is a flexible, self-settling armour unit that conforms to the seabed, absorbs wave and current energy, and stays put in turbulent water.

  • Rapid to fill and place — fill directly on site or off site for transport, then lift and position in a single crane movement.
  • Stable and self-locking — units deform to match the substrate and interlock with neighbouring bags.
  • Environmentally positive — the interstices between rocks become habitat for plants and marine life.
  • Fully recoverable — lift and relocate or remove units when used for temporary works.
Applications

One unit. Every scour problem.

From flash-flood emergency response to permanent offshore cable protection, Rock Bag Filter Units are engineered for above-ground and sub-surface use in fresh or saline environments.

Bridge Piers & Foundations

Shield structural supports from fierce currents and sediment displacement, without dewatering or heavy civil works.

Coastal Defence

Attenuate wave energy to protect shorelines, dunes, tidal inlets and sand-nourishment sites — while creating habitat.

Ports, Harbours & Seawalls

Toe protection, revetment reinforcement and scour resistance around pylons, wharves and quay walls.

Offshore Wind & Energy

Secure monopiles and turbine foundations against scour with units placed rapidly from vessel or barge.

Subsea Cables & Pipelines

Stabilise, weigh down and protect cable and pipeline crossings, tie-ins and free spans on any seabed.

Flood & Emergency Works

Deploy in routine or emergency situations — flash flooding, washouts and urgent asset protection.

Installation

Three steps from delivery to protection.

Fill

Fill units with locally sourced graded rock — directly on site, or at another location for transport. Filling frames keep the process fast and repeatable.

Lift

A single certified central lifting ring means one connection per unit. Handle with excavator, crane or vessel — 1 t units up to 12 t units.

Place

Position above ground or sub-surface. Units self-settle, conform to the substrate and interlock. Recover and reuse them when works are temporary.
