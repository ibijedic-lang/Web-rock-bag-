Why Rock Bag Filter Units

Proven protection with minimal equipment and manpower

A small team can efficiently install hundreds of units per day. The flexible units conform to the seabed or riverbed, absorb wave energy and stay stable in turbulent water.

Rapid Installation

Filled on site by volume and placed with standard lifting equipment — hundreds of units per day with a small crew. No formwork, no curing, no specialist plant.

Stable & Durable

Effective in turbulent, high-energy water. Suitable above ground or sub-surface, in fresh or saline environments, for temporary or long-term works.

Environmentally Friendly

The interstices between rocks create habitat for plants and marine life, so units integrate into the environment instead of sealing it off.

Lower Whole-of-Life Cost

Faster and more economical than concrete, gabions or riprap — and units can be lifted, relocated and reused as project needs change.

Flexible by Design

Units mould themselves to uneven beds, scour holes and structures — maintaining intimate contact where rigid solutions leave gaps.

Worldwide Supply

Supplied globally from our Queensland base, with deployment support, technical assistance and filling-station options for your project.

Unit Sizes

Materials That Last

HDPE netting, engineered for the harshest environments

Our netting uses high-density polyethylene with carbon black for superior UV resistance — maintaining strength and integrity over decades of sun, salt and abrasion.

  • Higher tensile strength than standard PE
  • Superior resistance to UV, temperature swings and abrasion
  • Up to 95% lower microplastic release than recycled alternatives
  • 50–100 year lifespan with UV stabiliser
