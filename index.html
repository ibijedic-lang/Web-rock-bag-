The Product

What is a Rock Bag Filter Unit?

A Rock Bag Filter Unit is a high-strength HDPE mesh net filled with quarried rock, closed with an integrated single-point lifting system. The result is a heavy, flexible unit that moulds itself to the seabed, riverbed or structure it protects — absorbing wave energy and staying stable in turbulent water where loose rock is washed away.

  • Suitable for above-ground or sub-surface installation
  • Performs in fresh or saline environments
  • Ideal for temporary works or permanent protection
  • UV, saltwater and abrasion resistant HDPE netting
  • Single-point lift for fast, accurate placement

Why Choose Rock Bags

Faster, more economical, more resilient

Small teams install hundreds of units a day — outperforming concrete, gabions and riprap on speed, cost and long-term stability.

Rapid Installation

Filled and placed with standard site equipment. Minimal manpower, minimal plant — hundreds of units installed per day by a small crew.

Proven Stability

Absorbs wave energy and stays put in turbulent water. Flexible units interlock and mould to the substrate instead of being undermined.

25–100+ Year Life

Engineered HDPE netting resists UV, saltwater and abrasion, with life expectancies from 25 to over 100 years depending on application.

Lower Whole-of-Life Cost

Less plant, less labour and locally sourced rock fill make rock bags more economical than concrete armour, gabions or riprap.

Environmentally Considerate

Natural rock fill and an open mesh structure allow marine life to colonise units, creating habitat rather than sterile hard armour.

Design Flexibility

Six standard sizes with defined dimensions, volumes and weights that drop straight into an engineer's design — temporary or permanent.

Installation

From delivery to deployed in four steps

Deliver Flat-Packed

Empty units ship compactly to site anywhere in the world, cutting transport cost and embodied carbon.

Fill With Local Rock

Bags are rapidly filled with locally sourced quarry rock using a simple filling frame and excavator or loader.

Single-Point Lift

The integrated lifting ring allows one-hook handling by crane or excavator for fast, accurate placement.

Place & Protect

Units mould to the bed and each other, forming a stable, permeable armour layer above or below the waterline.
