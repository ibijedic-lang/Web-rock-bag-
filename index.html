The Product

What is a Rock Bag Filter Unit?

A Rock Bag Filter Unit is a Raschel‑knitted, UV‑stabilised HDPE mesh bag filled with graded rock, engineered with internal vertical reinforcement ropes and a single lifting ring for fast, accurate placement. Developed over five years of product development and material testing, the concept is proven for designers and manufactured for the global market.

Engineered HDPE Mesh

Virgin high‑grade HDPE yarn with carbon black UV stabilisation — high tensile strength, superior resistance to abrasion, temperature and chemical exposure, and far less prone to microplastic breakdown than standard PE.

Single‑Point Lifting

Every size is proportionally designed with internal vertical reinforcement ropes and a single lifting ring, so units can be lifted, transported and placed quickly and accurately — by crane, excavator or vessel.

Stable in Turbulent Water

The flexible mass conforms to the seabed or riverbed, absorbs wave and current energy, and locks together with neighbouring units — remaining stable where rigid systems undermine and fail.

Habitat Friendly

The voids between rocks create marine habitat and allow natural sediment processes to continue — a softer, environmentally sympathetic alternative to concrete and rigid armour.

Applications

Flexible yet robust, rock bags have rapidly become the go‑to solution for erosion control and scour protection across construction and environmental markets — temporary or permanent, above ground or sub‑surface, fresh or saline.

Attenuate wave energy to protect shorelines, beaches, dunes, tidal inlets and sand‑nourishment sites with flexible, habitat‑friendly units rather than rigid structures.

Shield structural supports from fierce currents and sediment displacement — the proven remedy for bridge pier and abutment scour.

🛢️ Subsea Pipelines & Cables

Stabilise oil & gas pipelines, power cables and interconnectors, safeguarding essential infrastructure from movement, spanning and abrasion.

⚓ Ports, Harbours & Seawalls

Toe protection, revetment reinforcement and scour resistance around pylons, wharves and quay walls.

💧 Dams, Reservoirs & Spillways

Scour repair and protection from high flows and failures — including spillways, stilling basins, coffer dams and outlet works.

🚨 Flood & Asset Protection

Rapid response for flash flooding, riverbank stabilisation and protection of critical assets — deployable quickly with minimal plant.

Installation

From delivery to deployment in four steps.

Our circular filling jig increases daily output by 30% and keeps every bag in the best possible shape for safe lifting, transport and placement without damage. A small team can comfortably fill 100 bags per day.

  1. 1

    Set the frame

    Open the bag inside the purpose‑built circular filling frame — on site, or at a depot for transport.

  2. 2

    Fill with local rock

    Load graded, locally sourced rock by excavator or loader. No special fill material required.

  3. 3

    Close & lift

    Draw the closure and lift from the single ring — one point, one machine, one movement.

  4. 4

    Place with precision

    Position by crane, excavator or vessel, above water or subsea. Units nest together and stay put.
