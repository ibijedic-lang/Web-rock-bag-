Product Range

Rock Bag Filter Units are available in 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 tonne options. Units are filled by volume, not weight — the tonne rating simply identifies that the finished unit will, on average, fall within that weight range.

Netting Options

PE or HDPE — matched to your environment

Every unit is available with a netting specification suited to its service conditions:

  • HDPE with UV stabiliser — recommended for high-energy water, coastlines, and sensitive environments. 50–100 year lifespan.
  • Standard PE — an economical choice for temporary works and lower-exposure sites.
  • Carbon black throughout for superior UV resistance.
